# Attribution and scope

The MIT licence covers the repository's original software and documentation.
Existing copyright and licence notices remain in force, including the scorer
integration notice in `src/classifiers/LICENSE.argument_quality`.

Third-party datasets and model weights retain their own terms. The repository
includes compact derived analysis outputs, not the original AQR or DEBATE datasets
or model weights. These materials are not relicensed as MIT datasets. Consult the
original dataset terms before obtaining or reusing the underlying data.

The fine-tuned argument-quality scorer is distributed separately under its own
MIT licence and attribution notices:
https://huggingface.co/joshcyang/belief-engine-argument-quality-deberta-v3-large

The software citation follows the camera-ready manuscript title. The linked
arXiv preprint may display an earlier title.
